# Tree, search and jump to line

Each row is a property or a part of the schema: its name, its type, a "required" mark and its first rules. Click the arrow to open a row. A `$ref` opens in place, and the chip after the type names its target.

Select a row to see all of its rules and its full description on the right. Double-click it, or press Enter, to open its line in the schema text beside the docs.

Type a name in the search box to find a property anywhere in the schema and in the files it uses. Click a result to open the tree at that row.
